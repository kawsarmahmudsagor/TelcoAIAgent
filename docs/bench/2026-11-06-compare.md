## run_94 fixes (`after2/decline-*`)

The `--decline` script replays run_94's last turns: a purchase the balance can't cover, then "প্যাকেজটা এখন আমি নিতে চাচ্ছি না", then "না, আর কোন help লাগবে না। Thank you".

| Turn | run_94 (live) | Final prompt (4 runs) |
|---|---|---|
| Decline | "ঠিক আছে, এখন প্যাকেজটা নিচ্ছি না।" (the customer's voice) | "ঠিক আছে, তাহলে এখন থাক।" in 3 of 4; the customer's voice once |
| "No more help, thanks" | "স্বাগতম… আর কিছু জানতে চান?" | goodbye in 4 of 4 ("ধন্যবাদ, সামিহা… আল্লাহ হাফেজ।") |

The live-only parts of run_94 are fixed in code and Jev and have no bench equivalent:

- The filler that played on the decline: `quick_reply.route()` now trusts Jev when it is sure the turn needs nothing.
- The reminder that asked "anything else?" again: Jev's wrap-up criteria. `scripts/judge_bench/wrapup.py` scores 16/16, up from 10/16.
