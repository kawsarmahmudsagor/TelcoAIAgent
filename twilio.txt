Steps below follow Twilio's usual flow. The console layout changes often, so menu names may differ slightly.

1. Sign up

Go to https://www.twilio.com/try-twilio.
Enter your name, email and password, then verify your email.
Verify your phone number with an SMS code. Use the BD mobile the bot will call. It then becomes a Verified Caller ID, so trial calls to it work right away.
The onboarding questions (product, use case, language) don't matter. Pick Voice and Python.
2. Secure the account

Turn on two-factor authentication: profile menu → User settings.
The Account SID and Auth Token are on the console dashboard. The app doesn't need them, but keep them secret.
3. Upgrade (needed for a clean demo)

Admin / Billing → Upgrade account.
Add a card and prepay credit (about $20).
Card warning: many Bangladeshi cards block international online payments. Use a dual-currency/international card, or enable international transactions with your bank first.
Twilio may ask for identity or business details. Fill them in truthfully.
4. Buy one number

Phone Numbers → Manage → Buy a number.
Country: US (the simplest option). Capability: Voice.
Some countries' numbers require a regulatory address or bundle. US numbers usually don't.
5. Allow calls to Bangladesh

Voice → Settings → Geo Permissions → tick Bangladesh → Save.
If Bangladesh needs extra approval, submit the request there.
6. Elastic SIP Trunk (part of Phase 0)

Explore Products → Elastic SIP Trunking (pin it) → Create new trunk, name it realestate.
Termination: set the URI, e.g. realestate.pstn.twilio.com. Add a Credential List with a username and strong password you choose.
Numbers: attach the number from step 4.
7. Values for .env

TWILIO_PHONE_NUMBER: the number from step 4, in E.164 form (+1…).
TWILIO_SIP_TERMINATION_URI: the URI from step 6.
TWILIO_SIP_USERNAME and TWILIO_SIP_PASSWORD: the Credential List values.
Steps 1–5 alone get you a working account, a number, and permission to call Bangladesh. Step 6 is needed only when you start Phase 0.
